Third-party components shipped in balloon/engines/ or loaded from public CDNs:

- LibreDWG (via @mlightcad/libredwg-web) – GNU GPL v3. DWG → DXF conversion.
  If you SELL or DISTRIBUTE this tool to third parties, GPL obligations apply
  (you must offer the source code of the combined work). Hosting it as a web
  page for your own and your customers' use is the common interpretation of
  "not distributing", but check with a lawyer before selling licences.
  To remove it: delete libredwg-web.* from balloon/engines/ – DWG upload will
  then show the free-converter advice instead.
- OpenCascade (via occt-import-js) – LGPL 2.1. STEP reading.
- Tesseract OCR (via tesseract.js-core) + English model – Apache 2.0.
- pdf.js (Mozilla) – Apache 2.0.   jsPDF + jspdf-autotable – MIT.
- supabase-js – MIT.   Barlow / Barlow Condensed fonts – SIL OFL.
- qrcode-generator (Kazuhiko Arase) – MIT. QR codes on MMD tags / DC (apps/qr.js).
